Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jan 22, 2026

Summary

  • The app is designed to work entirely on-device
  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use advertising SDKs
  • We do not track you across apps and websites

Data the app uses

Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

If you grant permission, the app can read and write workout-related data (such as workout sessions and heart rate metrics) using Apple’s HealthKit framework so you can record and review your soccer sessions.

Health data access is controlled by you in iOS Settings:

  • Settings → Privacy & Security → Health

Location data (GPS)

If you grant permission, the app uses location services to record GPS routes during outdoor sessions and to generate route maps and movement heatmaps.

You can control location access in iOS Settings:

  • Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services

App settings and local files

The app stores certain settings (for example: preferences and thresholds) and may store locally saved session files on your device (including in an App Group container to support the Watch + iPhone experience).

Data sharing

The app does not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or tracking.

Data retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored app data at any time in the iPhone app:

  • Settings → Clear All Data

This removes locally saved sessions and profile/settings stored by the app on your device. It does not delete workouts already stored in Apple Health.

Children’s privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, visit the Support page or email umangd03@gmail.com.
