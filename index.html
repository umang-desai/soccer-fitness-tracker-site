How It Works

Start a session on iPhone or Apple Watch. For outdoor sessions, the app records GPS to build your route and movement heatmaps. If you allow it, sessions can be saved to Apple Health so your workouts stay in one place.

Tip: For best GPS accuracy, use Apple Watch outdoors with good sky view and keep Low Power Mode off during workouts.
