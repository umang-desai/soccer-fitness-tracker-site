Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a workout?

Open the app and tap the "Start" button on the home screen. Choose your session type (Match, Training, or Drill) and mode (Outdoor or Indoor), then tap Start to begin recording.

Why is my GPS not working?

Make sure you've granted location permissions to the app. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Soccer Fitness Tracker and select "Always" or "While Using App".

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

Install the app on your iPhone first, then open the Watch app on your iPhone and install Soccer Fitness Tracker to your Watch. You can start workouts directly from your wrist.

Why isn't my heart rate showing?

Heart rate requires HealthKit permissions. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Soccer Fitness Tracker and enable Heart Rate reading. For best results, use an Apple Watch.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Clear All Data". This removes all locally stored sessions and settings. Note: This does not delete workouts saved to Apple Health.

Is my data private?

Yes! All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
